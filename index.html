Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a solar installation take? ▼

Standard residential installations usually take between 1 to 3 days depending on system capacity and site structure.

How often should solar panels be cleaned? ▼

In urban areas like Lahore, washing panels every 2 to 4 weeks helps prevent dust buildup and ensures maximum power efficiency.

Do solar panels work during load-shedding? ▼

Hybrid and off-grid solar systems paired with battery storage continue supplying electricity even when the grid power is down.
